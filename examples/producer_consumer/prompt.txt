Implement a producer-consumer system with three components:

  1. A bounded FIFO queue of capacity 3. Items are positive integers in
     1..10. Enqueue is rejected when the queue is full; dequeue is rejected
     when the queue is empty.
  2. A producer that generates items 1..3 in order and enqueues them.
  3. A consumer that dequeues items and tracks the running sum.

Safety properties:
  - The queue length is always in 0..3.
  - No item is ever lost: every item produced is eventually consumed in
    FIFO order (i.e. consumer.received is a prefix of producer.generated).
  - The producer never produces beyond item 3.
