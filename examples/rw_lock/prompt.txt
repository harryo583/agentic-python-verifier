Implement a single-writer / multi-reader lock with 3 client threads.

Components:
  1. A lock with state in {"free","read","write"} and a non-negative
     reader counter (bounded at 3).
  2. Three clients, each in {"idle","reading","writing"}.

Safety properties:
  - Mutual exclusion: when state="write", reader_count=0 and at most one
    client is in "writing".
  - No torn state: if any client is "reading", state="read" and
    reader_count >= 1; if state="free", reader_count=0 and no client
    is reading or writing.
  - reader_count always equals the number of clients in state "reading".
