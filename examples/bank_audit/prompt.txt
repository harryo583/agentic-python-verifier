Implement a multi-account ledger with audit-log integrity.

Components:
  1. Two accounts A and B, each with a non-negative integer balance bounded
     by 10. The system starts with A=5, B=5.
  2. An append-only ledger of transfer entries. Each entry records
     (from, to, amount) and is added atomically with the balance change.
  3. A transfer action Transfer(from, to, amount) that debits the source
     and credits the destination, appending one ledger entry. Transfer is
     rejected if it would overdraft the source or overflow the destination.

Safety properties:
  - Conservation: the sum of all account balances is invariant (always 10).
  - Audit integrity: the sum over the ledger of (+amount to "to") minus
    (+amount from "from") equals the difference between current and initial
    balance for every account.
  - No account balance ever goes negative or above 10.
