Implement a single-leader replicated log with 3 nodes.

Components:
  1. Three nodes, each holding a sequence of integer log entries (entries
     drawn from 1..3, max log length 3) and a "term" integer (0..2).
  2. A leader election: exactly one node may have role "leader"; the
     others are "followers". Leadership can transfer by incrementing
     the term and electing a new leader.
  3. An AppendEntry action: the leader extends its own log with one
     entry; followers copy the leader's log on Replicate.

Safety properties:
  - Election safety: at most one leader per term.
  - Log matching: any follower's log is a prefix of the current leader's
    log (after Replicate stabilises).
  - Term monotonicity: term never decreases.
