Implement a two-phase commit coordinator with 2 resource managers.

Components:
  1. Two resource managers RM1 and RM2, each in one of:
     {"working","prepared","committed","aborted"}. Initially both "working".
  2. A coordinator that runs 2PC: it issues Prepare to each RM, collects
     votes, and decides Commit (if all voted prepared) or Abort.

Safety properties:
  - Agreement: no two RMs end in different terminal decisions
    (one committed, one aborted is forbidden).
  - A committed RM was preceded by a coordinator Commit decision.
  - No RM rolls back from "committed" to anything else.
